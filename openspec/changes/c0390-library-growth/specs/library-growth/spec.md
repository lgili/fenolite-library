## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Authored items come from public dimensions
Every authored footprint SHALL be generated by a Fenolite package generator from a record whose
dimensions are read from a public standard or manufacturer document. Each document SHALL be registered as a
D-id in `sources.toml` and listed in the item's `PROVENANCE.json` with its URL, title, revision and date.
No third-party CAD file, drawing or 3D model SHALL be copied.

#### Scenario: A chip resistor family is added
- **WHEN** the 0402 to 2512 resistor rows are expanded from the family table
- **THEN** each item's record cites the registered D-id of the datasheet that gives its body and terminal ranges

#### Scenario: A family has no generator
- **WHEN** a package family has no c0260 generator
- **THEN** no item is drawn pad by pad and the gap is reported as a generator proposal

### Requirement: Families are generated from tables
Items of one package family SHALL be produced from one parameter table, one row per package, by a
deterministic expander. Running the expander twice SHALL give byte-identical item folders.

#### Scenario: Expander repeated
- **WHEN** the expander runs twice on the same table
- **THEN** every written file is byte-identical

### Requirement: Every item is proved before it enters
An item SHALL enter only when its record reads back with no error finding, its generated outputs equal a
fresh regeneration byte for byte, its review sheet renders, and the library audit of the item passes.
A failing item SHALL be fixed or left out, never waived.

#### Scenario: Regeneration differs
- **WHEN** a committed `.kicad_mod` differs from the planned regeneration
- **THEN** the batch does not enter until the output is regenerated from the record

### Requirement: Imports only under accepted licences
An imported item SHALL come from an upstream whose licence was read in its repository and is on the
allow-list. It SHALL pin the upstream commit, keep the upstream notices unchanged and stay in the folder of
its licence family. A source with an unclear licence SHALL be left out and listed for the maintainer.

#### Scenario: A repository has a modified licence text
- **WHEN** a candidate library adds its own exception to a share-alike licence
- **THEN** it is recorded as unclear and no item from it enters
